📅 How to use this

  • Each lesson is a real 30 minutes — press ▶ Start lesson at the top of the page and the built-in timer keeps you paced.
  • The timer pauses if you switch tabs, so it counts only real working time (no speeding through!).
  • At 30 minutes a 🎉 Break screen pops up — take 10–15 minutes, then come back and do a second lesson for a full hour.
  • Read the lesson, do every "Try it" and practice set, then take the quiz and click Check my answers.
  • Star ⭐ Art is open for free drawing anytime you want a creative break.
🗓️
Suggested daily rhythm: one 30-minute lesson → 10–15 min break → a second 30-minute lesson. That's your hour!

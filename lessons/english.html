🧩 The 8 Parts of Speech

PartIts jobExamples
NounNames a person, place, thing, or ideaartist, studio, courage
PronounReplaces a nounshe, it, they, we
VerbShows action or beingpaint, run, is, became
AdjectiveDescribes a nounbright, three, curious
AdverbDescribes a verb, adjective, or adverb (often -ly)quickly, very, often
PrepositionShows position or relationshipunder, before, with
ConjunctionJoins words or ideasand, but, because
InterjectionShows sudden emotionWow! Oh no! Yes!
🎨
Art connection: "The curious (adj) artist boldly (adv) painted (verb) a mural (noun)." Every colorful word is doing a job!

🏗️ Building a Complete Sentence

Subject + Predicate

Subject = who or what the sentence is about.
Predicate = what they do or are (contains the verb).

"My older sister paints every weekend."

ProblemWhat it isFix
FragmentMissing a subject or verb. "Because she loves art."Finish the thought: "She joined the club because she loves art."
Run-onTwo sentences jammed together. "She paints she also sketches."Split or join properly: "She paints, and she also sketches."

📚 Read & Answer

Read the passage carefully, then answer the questions below.

Maya had always loved color, but she had never tried painting a mural. When her art teacher asked for volunteers to decorate the school's empty hallway, Maya's hand shot up before she could second-guess herself. For two weeks she sketched, mixed paints, and climbed ladders after school.

The morning the mural was revealed, students stopped and stared. A giant sunrise spread across the wall in warm oranges, balanced by a cool blue sky. Maya felt nervous and proud at the same time. She realized that the scariest projects often became the ones she was most glad she tried.

1What is the passage mainly about?
2Raising her hand "before she could second-guess herself" shows Maya is…
3Which sentence best states the theme (message)?

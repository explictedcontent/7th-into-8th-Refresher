Science isn't just facts to memorize — it's a method for figuring out how the world works. Learn the steps real scientists use on every experiment.

🔁 The Scientific Method

  1. Question — What are you curious about? ("Does music help plants grow?")
  2. Research — Find out what's already known.
  3. Hypothesis — A testable, "If… then…" prediction.
  4. Experiment — Test it, changing only one thing at a time.
  5. Analyze — Collect data and look for patterns.
  6. Conclusion — Was your hypothesis supported? Share your results.
💡
A good hypothesis is testable. Format: "If I play music for my plant, then it will grow taller than a plant with no music."

🎚️ Variables: the heart of a fair test

VariableWhat it meansPlant example
IndependentThe ONE thing you changeWhether music plays
DependentWhat you measure (it depends on the change)How tall the plant grows
ControlledEverything you keep the same to be fairWater, sunlight, soil, pot size
🎯
Memory tip: I change the Independent variable. The Dependent variable Depends on it.

🧪 Lab Safety Rules

  • Wear safety goggles whenever told to
  • Never eat or drink in the lab
  • Tie back long hair and roll up loose sleeves
  • Tell your teacher about any spill or accident right away
  • Know where the sink, fire extinguisher, and exit are
  • Never mix chemicals unless instructed
⚠️
Golden rule: when in doubt, ask the teacher before you act — not after.

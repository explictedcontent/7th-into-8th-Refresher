🌅 Warm-up · Look closely

Artists notice details most people walk past. Look around the room you're in right now and find one example of each. Say them out loud or jot them down:

  • A line (the edge of a table, a cord, a stripe)
  • A shape (a window, a screen, a sign)
  • A warm color (red, orange, or yellow)
  • A cool color (blue, green, or purple)
  • Something with interesting texture (rough, soft, bumpy)

🧱 The 7 Elements of Art

Think of these like the ingredients in a recipe. Mix them differently and you get completely different art.

ElementWhat it isSpot it in real life
LineA mark with length and directionThe edge of a phone, your signature
ShapeA flat, enclosed area (2-D)A road sign, an emoji
FormA shape with depth (3-D)A basketball, a sculpture
ColorHue made by reflected lightA sunset, a paint set
ValueHow light or dark something isShadows on a face
TextureHow a surface feels (or looks like it feels)Tree bark, soft fur
SpaceThe area around and between objectsThe gap that makes a drawing look far away

🌈 Color Theory Basics

The color wheel

  • Primary colors — red, yellow, blue. You can't mix other colors to make these.
  • Secondary colors — orange, green, purple. Mix two primaries to get one.
  • Complementary colors sit opposite on the wheel (like blue & orange). Side by side, they really pop!

Warm vs. Cool

Warm 🔥 reds, oranges, yellows — feel cozy, exciting, close.
Cool ❄️ blues, greens, purples — feel calm, quiet, far away.

🖌️ Studio Challenge · "Complementary Sunset"

Time to use everything you learned. Follow the steps below in your studio. Don't rush — take your time and make it yours. 🎨

  1. Draw a straight horizon line across the middle (that's the Line element).
  2. Add a big round sun using a warm color (red, orange, or yellow).
  3. Fill the sky around it with a cool complementary color (blue or purple) so the sun pops.
  4. Show value: make the sky darker at the top and lighter near the sun.
  5. Add texture to the ground — short strokes for grass, or waves for water.
  6. Hit 💾 Save to keep your masterpiece!

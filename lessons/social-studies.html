Map Skills, Continents & Coordinates 🗺️

Before you study history or current events, you need to find places on a map. These skills let you pinpoint any spot on Earth.

🎯 What you'll learn

  • The 7 continents and 5 oceans
  • How to read a map's compass rose, key, and scale
  • Latitude and longitude — Earth's address system
  • The special lines: Equator and Prime Meridian

🌍 Continents & Oceans

There are 7 continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, and South America.

And 5 oceans: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern (Antarctic), and Arctic.

💡
Did you know? Asia is the largest continent and the Pacific is the largest ocean — so big it covers about a third of the planet!

🧭 Reading a Map

ToolWhat it does
Compass roseShows directions: N, S, E, W (and NE, SW…)
Map key / legendExplains what the symbols and colors mean
ScaleShows how map distance compares to real distance
TitleTells you what the map is about
🧭
Remember the directions: "Never Eat Soggy Waffles" = North, East, South, West (clockwise).

📍 Latitude & Longitude

These imaginary lines form a grid so every place has an exact "address."

LinesDirectionMeasured from0° line
LatitudeRun east–west (but measure north/south)How far N or SThe Equator
LongitudeRun north–south (but measure east/west)How far E or WThe Prime Meridian
🪜
Trick: Latitude lines are like the rungs of a ladder (flat). Always say latitude first, like "40° N, 74° W" for New York City.

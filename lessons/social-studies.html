🌍 Continents & Oceans

There are 7 continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, and South America.

And 5 oceans: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern (Antarctic), and Arctic.

💡
Did you know? Asia is the largest continent and the Pacific is the largest ocean — so big it covers about a third of the planet!

📚 Why Geography Matters

Long before phones could show a blue dot on a map, explorers crossed oceans using only the stars, a compass, and lines of latitude and longitude. Knowing exactly where you are has always shaped human history — where cities grew, which countries traded, and how armies moved.

Geography still shapes daily life. Mountains and rivers decide where roads and farms can go. A country near the Equator has a very different climate from one near the poles. When you understand maps, the news about faraway places suddenly makes a lot more sense.

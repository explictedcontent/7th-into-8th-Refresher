➕➖ Part 1: Integers

Integers are whole numbers and their negatives: … −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3 … (no fractions or decimals). Picture them on a number line — negatives to the left, positives to the right.

The sign rules

SituationRuleExample
Same signs, addAdd, keep the sign−4 + (−3) = −7
Different signs, addSubtract, keep the bigger number's sign−9 + 4 = −5
Multiply / divide — same signsAnswer is positive−6 × −2 = 12
Multiply / divide — different signsAnswer is negative−6 × 2 = −12
💡
Subtracting = adding the opposite. 5 − (−3) becomes 5 + 3 = 8. Two minuses next to each other turn into a plus!

🧭 Part 2: Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

When a problem has many steps, everyone has to solve it in the same order — or we'd all get different answers. Use PEMDAS:

  1. Parentheses (and other grouping symbols) first
  2. Exponents (powers & roots)
  3. Multiplication & Division — left to right
  4. Addition & Subtraction — left to right
⚠️
Watch out! Multiply and divide are a team — do whichever comes first, left to right. Same for add and subtract. Don't always do all multiplication before division!
Worked example

Solve 6 + 2 × (5 − 1)²

  1. Parentheses: 5 − 1 = 4  →  6 + 2 × 4²
  2. Exponent: 4² = 16  →  6 + 2 × 16
  3. Multiply: 2 × 16 = 32  →  6 + 32
  4. Add: 6 + 32 = 38
